How we build

Principles in every release

  • Private by design

    Your files and data stay on your device. Our tools don't ask for accounts they don't need.

  • Offline-first

    Most of our apps work without an internet connection — on a plane, underground, anywhere.

  • Small & fast

    Lean builds that install quickly, launch instantly and respect your storage and battery.

  • Actively maintained

    Every app in our catalogue received updates in 2026. We fix issues and keep improving.

The studio

Made with care by an independent team.

Anjneya Tech is a small, independent mobile studio with a simple belief: a great app doesn't need to be complicated to be genuinely useful.

We pick everyday problems worth solving — reading files, sharing photos safely, quick calculations, a good puzzle on the commute — and build the lightest, most thoughtful app we can. No dark patterns, no clutter.
