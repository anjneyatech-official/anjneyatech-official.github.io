Account and data deletion

Effective 9 October 2026

In short: Anjneya Tech apps do not create accounts and do not store your data on our servers. Your data lives on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time. You can also ask us by email and we will act within 30 days.

AdLens (com.ajtech.adlens) by Anjneya Tech

AdLens does not create its own accounts. You sign in with your existing Google account, and all app data (cached AdMob reports, settings and sign-in tokens) is stored only on your device.

Delete your data yourself, immediately

  1. Revoke AdLens's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This stops AdLens from reading your AdMob data.
  2. Sign out in the app (Settings, then Sign out) to remove stored sign-in tokens.
  3. Uninstall the app to erase all data stored on the device.

What is deleted

  • Cached AdMob reports and preferences, removed when you uninstall.
  • Google Sign-In tokens on the device, removed when you sign out.
  • AdLens's permission to read your AdMob data, removed when you revoke access.

Nothing is kept by us after these steps, because nothing is sent to us. Your AdMob account and its data belong to Google. Manage them in your AdMob account and Google Account.

All other apps and games

OpenAny, QRino, CleanShare, OneCalc, Chess Clock Pro, Inkroad, Loop The Loop, Slide Out and Color Escape have no accounts. To delete their data, open Android Settings, then Apps, choose the app, then Storage and Clear storage, or simply uninstall it.

In ad-supported apps, data used for ads is held by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads).

Ask us to delete data

If you have emailed us and want those messages deleted, or have any other deletion request, email us with the subject "Delete my data" and the name of the app. We confirm and complete requests within 30 days, and may keep a minimal record of the request itself where the law requires it.
