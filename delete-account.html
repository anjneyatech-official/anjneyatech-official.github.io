Account and data deletion

Last updated: July 2026

AdLens (com.ajtech.adlens)

AdLens does not create its own accounts and does not store your data on our servers. You sign in with your existing Google account, and all app data (cached AdMob reports, settings and sign-in tokens) is stored only on your device.

1. Delete your data yourself (immediate)

  1. Revoke AdLens's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This stops AdLens from reading your AdMob data.
  2. Sign out in the app (Settings → Sign out) to remove stored authentication tokens.
  3. Uninstall the app to erase all locally stored data.

2. Request deletion by email

Prefer we handle it? Email us with the subject "Delete my data". We action all requests within 30 days.

Request deletion by email

3. What gets deleted

  • Locally cached AdMob reports and preferences (removed on uninstall).
  • Google Sign-In / OAuth tokens on the device (removed on sign-out).
  • AdLens's authorization to access your AdMob data (removed on revoke).

Note: your AdMob account and its data are owned and controlled by Google, not by AdLens. Manage that data through your AdMob account and Google Account.
