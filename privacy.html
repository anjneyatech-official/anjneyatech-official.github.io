1. Who we are and what this covers

Anjneya Tech ("we", "us", "our") is an independent developer of mobile apps and games based in India. This policy applies to our apps and games on Google Play (OpenAny, QRino, CleanShare, OneCalc, Chess Clock Pro, AdLens, Inkroad, Loop The Loop, Slide Out and Color Escape), to any future app that links to it, and to this website (together, the "Services").

Anjneya Tech is the data controller, or "data fiduciary" under Indian law, for the limited personal data described here. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through Google Play, Google Sign-In and Google AdMob.

2. Information we collect

Content on your device. Files you open, photos you clean, QR codes you scan or create, calculations, settings and game progress are processed and stored on your device. We do not receive, upload or store this content.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

Diagnostics from Google Play. If you allow your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give us aggregated crash and performance reports (Android vitals). These help us fix bugs and do not identify you to us.

Advertising data. In ad-supported apps, Google AdMob collects certain device data to show and measure ads. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. See section 4.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as health, financial account, biometric or precise location data. Values you type into calculators, such as loan amounts or body measurements, stay on your device.

4. Advertising

AdLens, Loop The Loop, Slide Out and Color Escape show ads served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and cap ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google handles this data under its own policies: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are served in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy and you can choose whether to allow personalised ads. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (usually Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads).

We publish an app-ads.txt file so that ads are sold only through sellers we have authorised. We do not choose or control individual ads. If you see one that seems inappropriate, tell us and we will report or block it.

5. Third-party services

We use only these third-party services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play for distribution, updates and crash reports (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob for ads in ad-supported apps (how Google uses advertising data)
  • Google Sign-In and the AdMob API in AdLens only
  • GitHub Pages to host this website (GitHub Privacy Statement)

Our apps may let you reach other websites, for example through a link in a QR code or a file. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

6. Device permissions

Our apps ask only for permissions needed for the features you use, for example the camera to scan a QR code in QRino. Android asks for your permission first, and you can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. Revoking a permission may stop the related feature from working.

7. Why we process data (legal bases)

Where the GDPR, the UK GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the app features you choose to use.
  • Legitimate interests: to fix bugs and keep the Services secure using aggregated diagnostics, and to answer your messages.
  • Consent: for personalised advertising where consent is required. You can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect earlier processing.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we process personal data only for the purposes in this policy, based on your consent or on the legitimate uses that Act permits.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Emails you send us are kept while we handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless the law requires longer. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Data collected by Google is kept under Google's policies.

To ask us to delete data, see Account and data deletion or email us.

9. Security

Keeping data on your device rather than on our servers is our main safeguard. We also rely on the security features of Android and Google Play, and this website is served only over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock.

10. International transfers

We do not transfer your personal data ourselves. Google and GitHub may process data in other countries, including the United States, using the safeguards described in their own policies, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Children

The Services are meant for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Where Indian law applies, we do not carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct, update or complete it;
  • have it deleted;
  • object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent;
  • data portability;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (India);
  • complain to a data protection authority or the Data Protection Board of India.

Because we hold very little personal data, most requests are met by deleting app data on your device. For anything else, email us. We reply within 30 days and may first need to confirm your identity. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

13. Region-specific information

United States, including California. We do not sell personal information for money. In ad-supported apps, AdMob's use of device identifiers for targeted ads may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under some state laws. You can opt out by deleting or resetting your advertising ID, or turning off personalised ads, in your device settings. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. You have the rights in section 12 and may complain to your local supervisory authority.

India. You have the rights given by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Please raise a grievance with us first, as described in section 16, before approaching the Data Protection Board of India.

14. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and loads no content from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to secure and operate its service.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps, the law or our practices change. We will post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we handle your personal data, we will tell you in the relevant app or its release notes and, where required, ask for your consent.

16. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, write to our Grievance Officer at anjneyatech@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We acknowledge grievances promptly and aim to resolve them within 30 days.
