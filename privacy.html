Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps and games published by Anjneya Tech ("we", "us", "our") on Google Play: OpenAny, QRino, CleanShare, OneCalc, Chess Clock Pro, AdLens, Inkroad, Loop The Loop, Slide Out and Color Escape.

In short: our apps do their work on your device. We don't run servers that collect your personal data, we never sell data, and six of our ten apps show no ads at all.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information for our own use. The files you open, photos you clean, calculations you make, QR codes you create and your game progress are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive this data.

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports (Android vitals). These do not identify you and help us fix bugs.

2. Advertising

Apps with ads: Loop The Loop, Slide Out, Color Escape and AdLens show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Ad-free apps: OpenAny, QRino, CleanShare, OneCalc, Chess Clock Pro and Inkroad show no ads.

We publish an app-ads.txt file (IAB Tech Lab standard) so ads are only served through authorised sellers.

3. AdLens and Google Sign-In

AdLens lets you sign in with your Google account to read your own AdMob reports through Google's APIs. Your reports, settings and sign-in tokens are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. You can revoke access at any time; see Account and data deletion.

4. Permissions

Our apps only request permissions needed for the features you use, for example camera access to scan a QR code in QRino. You can review or revoke permissions in your device's Settings at any time.

5. Children's privacy

Our apps and games are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Play Services: app distribution, updates and crash reporting
  • Google AdMob: advertising (ad-supported apps only)
  • Google Sign-In: AdLens only

7. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstalling an app removes all data it stored on your device.
  • To ask a question or request deletion of any data, see Account and data deletion or email us.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
