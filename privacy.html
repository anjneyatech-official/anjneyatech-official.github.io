Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Anjneya Tech ("we", "us", or "our") operates mobile applications available on the Google Play Store. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our apps.

1. Information We Collect

Our apps are designed to minimize data collection. We may collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version) for crash reporting
  • Game progress and scores stored locally on your device
  • Anonymized usage analytics to improve gameplay experience

2. Advertising

Our apps display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

We participate in the IAB Tech Lab's app-ads.txt program to ensure ads are served through authorized channels. You can view our app-ads.txt file here.

3. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

4. Data Storage

Game data is stored locally on your device. We do not store personal information on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — Advertising
  • Google Play Services — App distribution and updates

6. Your Rights

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization). To request deletion of any data associated with our apps, see Account & Data Deletion.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at anjneyatech@gmail.com.
